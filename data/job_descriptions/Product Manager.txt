# Product Manager

**Job Description:**

We are looking for a motivated and strategic Product Manager to manage the product development lifecycle, understand customer requirements, and deliver successful digital products. The ideal candidate should have excellent communication, leadership, analytical, and problem-solving skills.

### Key Responsibilities:
- Define product vision, strategy, and roadmap.
- Gather and analyze customer requirements.
- Collaborate with engineering, design, and marketing teams.
- Create product requirements documents (PRDs).
- Prioritize product features based on business needs.
- Monitor product performance and user feedback.
- Manage product development timelines and milestones.
- Coordinate product launches and improvements.
- Conduct market research and competitor analysis.
- Ensure products meet customer expectations and business objectives.

### Required Skills:
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
- Knowledge of product management principles.
- Understanding of Agile and Scrum methodologies.
- Experience with product roadmaps and backlog management.
- Knowledge of market research and business analysis.
- Familiarity with Jira, Trello, or Asana.
- Understanding of software development lifecycle (SDLC).
- Knowledge of wireframing and prototyping tools such as Figma.
- Strong leadership and decision-making abilities.
- Analytical and problem-solving skills.

### Preferred Qualifications:
- Knowledge of SQL and data analytics.
- Familiarity with Google Analytics and Power BI.
- Understanding of UI/UX design principles.
- Knowledge of software development and APIs.
- Experience with A/B testing and product metrics.
- Familiarity with cloud-based software products.
- Understanding of customer relationship management (CRM).

### Education:
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- MBA or Product Management certification is an advantage.

### Experience:
- 0–2 years of experience.
- Freshers with product-related academic projects, internships, or business analysis experience are welcome.
